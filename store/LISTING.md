## English (en-us)

**Short description**
Eye breaks every 20 minutes and a stretch break every hour, with video and voice guidance.

**What's new in this version (1.2.0)**
• New Dashboard: your break score, screen time, eye and stretch breaks for today, 7 or 30 days, compared with the period before.
• Insights from your own history, such as the time of day you skip breaks most.
• Export a one-page PDF report to show your optometrist.
• The tray icon can count down the minutes to your next break.
• Settings now tell you how many breaks a full shift gives you.

**Description**
ScreenRest is a free break coach for people who work at a screen all day.

• Eye breaks that follow the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds, with a short guided exercise.
• A 5-minute stretch break every hour: stand up, follow short video demos for your neck, shoulders, back and legs, then walk and drink water.
• Calm voice guidance, so your eyes can leave the screen.
• Follows your shift, work days and lunch hour. Waits while you're on a call or presenting, and pauses while you keep typing.
• A Dashboard that shows how you're doing today, this week or this month: break score, screen time, breaks per day, the time of day you take them, and short insights from your own history.
• A quick check-in at the end of your shift for eye blur and energy, and a one-page PDF report to show your optometrist.
• Optional countdown to your next break on the tray icon.
• English or Tagalog interface.
• Private: no account, no ads, no internet connection. Your history stays on your PC as plain files. Open source (MIT).

ScreenRest is a wellness reminder, not medical advice.
