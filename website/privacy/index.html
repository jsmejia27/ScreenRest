ScreenRest Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

In short: ScreenRest collects no information, never connects to the internet, and keeps everything on your own computer.

ScreenRest (also listed as "Screen Rest" in the Microsoft Store) is a free, open-source break reminder for Windows and macOS made by Jilbert Mejia. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information we collect

None. ScreenRest does not collect personal information. It has no accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics, tracking or crash reporting.

Information stored on your computer

The app saves the following only on your own computer, in the folder %APPDATA%\ScreenRest on Windows or ~/Library/Application Support/ScreenRest on a Mac:

  • Your settings: work hours, break times, language and similar choices.
  • Your daily break counts and active time.
  • Your optional daily check-in answers, which rate eye blur and energy.

This information never leaves your computer. You can open the folder from the app (About, then Open data folder), and you can delete it at any time. On Windows, uninstalling the app can remove it too; on a Mac, delete the folder after moving the app to the Trash.

Internet use

ScreenRest does not connect to the internet. It works fully offline and does not send any information to us or to anyone else.

Camera, microphone and activity

To avoid interrupting you, ScreenRest checks whether you're on a call or presenting, and whether you're using your keyboard or mouse:

  • Camera and microphone: the app checks whether they are in use, from the record Windows keeps or the device status macOS reports. It never turns on, records from or listens to your camera or microphone.
  • Keyboard and mouse: the app reads only how long it has been since your last input. It never records what you type or click.

Children

ScreenRest collects no information from anyone, including children.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any information, because we don't have any.

This website

screenrest.jilbert.net is a static website with no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, and downloads come from GitHub, so those services receive the usual technical details (such as your IP address) when your browser contacts them.

Open source

You can check all of this yourself in the app's source code: github.com/jsmejia27/ScreenRest

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
